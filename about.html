Our History

Founded in 1990 by Craig Mapungwana "Samahz", Samahz FC began as a humble neighborhood team struggling to make ends meet. Through sheer determination and passion for the game, we gradually rose through Zimbabwe's football ranks.

Our breakthrough came in 2005 when we earned promotion to the top flight, marking the start of our golden era. Since then, we've established ourselves as a dominant force in Zimbabwean football, winning multiple league titles and domestic cups.

Today, Samahz FC has grown beyond borders, competing in continental tournaments and earning international recognition. Our journey from grassroots to global recognition stands as testament to the power of perseverance and vision.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Community Focus:

"To be a vital part of our community, fostering a spirit of pride and belonging through football."

Athletic Excellence:

"To consistently strive for the highest levels of play and performance, inspiring our players and fans."

Inspiring Fans:

"To create unforgettable experiences for our fans, building a strong connection through the passion of the game."

Developing Talent:

"To nurture and develop young talent, providing opportunities for growth and success on and off the field."

Social Impact:

"To use the power of football to make a positive impact on our community and society."

Our Vision

Top-Tier Status:

"To become a leading club in our league, competing for championships and earning international recognition."

Global Brand:

"To establish ourselves as a globally recognized and respected football club, inspiring fans worldwide."

Sustainability and Innovation:

"To be a sustainable and innovative club, leading the way in responsible football practices and technologies."

Player Development:

"To be a renowned player development center, producing top-tier talent for national and international teams."

Fan Engagement:

"To cultivate a passionate and engaged fan base, creating a vibrant and inclusive atmosphere at our stadium and online."
